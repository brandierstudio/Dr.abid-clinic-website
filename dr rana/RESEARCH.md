# Design aur source research

## Original brand aur material
- Official source: https://drranaobaiddentalsurgery.com/ — homepage, About, Services, General, Cosmetic Dentistry, Surgical Dentistry, Contact aur New Patient pages check kiye gaye.
- Logo: `public/images/36d4c3e355-768x576.png` (original graphic), extra brand mark `public/images/wwwm11-6-300x71.png`.
- Original imagery local copy: `public/images/`; is mein Dr. Rana ka asal portrait, clinic photo, original patient graphics aur teen service photos shamil hain. Koi AI-generated ya generic stock photo istemal nahin karni.
- Brand evidence: original page loader deep blue `#062c78` use karta hai; logo mein peela/golden tooth mark nazar aata hai. Redesign isi blue/gold identity ko maintain karega.
- Homepage bio, services aur Karachi branches/timings ko preference di gayi hai kyun ke yeh user ke diye gaye source URL par sab se naya displayed clinic-specific detail set hai. Original subpage footer mein mazeed ek address (“Hanif Hospital, Block B, Matric Board Office Flyover, North Nazimabad, Karachi”) bhi diya gaya hai, jo homepage ke Taj Medical Complex aur Clifton addresses se mukhtalif hai; prototype ise unverified source discrepancy ke taur par label karta hai. Homepage par Defence branch ka phone number hai, magar physical address nahin diya gaya.
- Original menu `Home, About, Services, General, Cosmetic Dentistry, Surgical Dentistry, Contact, New Patient` hai; prototype mein in routes ko local pages mein map kiya gaya hai. Appointment aur phone/email/Google Maps links bhi original destinations/data ke mutabiq rakhe gaye hain.

## External design references
- Lasso MD, “Top 20 Best Dental Websites of 2026”: mobile-first UX, intuitive navigation, real practice imagery aur zaroori jagah par clear phone/location/appointment pathways. https://www.lassomd.com/blog/top-10-best-dental-websites
- MediBoost, “15 Dental Web Design Ideas”: animated patient journeys, interactive FAQ, carefully used visuals aur mobile usability ke ideas. https://www.mediboost.com.au/website-design-inspiration/
- UserWay, “Dental Websites: Accessibility & Engagement Best Practices”: keyboard accessibility, readable contrast, descriptive links, responsive layout aur form usability. https://userway.org/blog/dental-websites-accessibility/

References se visual inspiration li gayi hai; clinic ke liye naya fact, testimonial, award ya medical claim invent nahin kiya jayega. Motion optional/reduced-motion friendly hogi aur design asli brand se bandha rahega.
